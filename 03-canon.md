# DECISION CANON (template)

> Every major owner decision is recorded WITH the rejected alternatives.
> This is what stops the team from re-inventing an already-rejected idea a month later.

## YYYY-MM-DD · Decision title
**Context:** why the decision was needed.
**Decision:** what was chosen.
**Rejected alternatives:** (a) … — why not; (b) … — why not.
**Source:** diary entry / discussion link.
